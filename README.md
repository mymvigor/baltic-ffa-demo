# Baltic FFA Dashboard

## Basis and Correlation update

The four page sections are Forecast, Spot Market, Forward Market and Correlation. If the live page still says Market Research, it is not this version.

Price Explorer adds Basis / Basis % modes to the same main chart and Series/Time Axis/Frequency controls. Basis = BFA − same-date compatible Spot; Basis % = 100 × Basis / positive Spot. Positive values mean forward premium, negative values discount, with no undervaluation or zero-terminal-basis claim. Absolute basis needs compatible quote units; percentage bases can compare dimensionless relative premiums. Original prices and dates remain available, and rolls stay unadjusted with breaks/markers.

Each selected basis has a compact latest level/change caption and annual historical context: one closest real observation per prior quote year, within ±7 calendar days, same benchmark/unit/tenor, delivery season for fixed contracts and exact integer months to delivery. Percentiles exclude the current observation, use midranks for ties and require five annual counterparts. These are descriptive, not trading probabilities. In-delivery monthly contracts may show (all settlement sessions × BFA − published Spot sum) / remaining sessions, only with supported contract rules and a verified complete calendar. Missing known Spot prevents the estimate. Forecast settlement minus current BFA is labelled Valuation Gap, not basis. Quarter/year remaining-Spot estimates are not inferred.

Correlation reuses the searchable inventory, source frames, seasonal coordinates and date-axis components. Core controls are Series, Alignment, Data Type, Frequency and View. Cross-benchmark loading is optional in Series scope. Factor values are computed only for selected entries; choosing two raw compatible BFA legs offers their near-minus-far Spread in the same Series picker, without generating a quadratic menu. Existing OOS validation is explicitly enabled under Existing signal validation; ordinary correlations do not train a new model. No new regression or PCA is added.

Objects include raw Spot/Fixed/Rolling, explicit Spot/Rolling quote years, Basis, Basis %, Basis Change, MA5, MA20, MA5 − MA20, Momentum 5D and Volatility 20D. MA counts valid same-contract quotes; momentum requires five complete intervening native sessions; volatility is the sample SD of twenty absolute same-contract daily changes, not annualized. Data Type applies to the selected factor too; Price / Level retains factor values directly. Percentage changes require positive prior levels.

Matrix prefers all-series common valid samples; when weekday/coverage differences leave fewer than 30 shared observations, it uses exact pairwise valid dates and shows N per cell, without ranking coefficients from different samples. Rolling correlations use the selected rolling period and at least min(30, window) paired observations, with endpoint source dates and N on hover. One or a few rolling estimates cannot establish stability. Level correlation carries a shared-trend/contract-repricing caution; change and future-label calculations exclude roll jumps and do not fill quotes.

Lead/Lag uses Signal(t) versus Target(t+h) − Target(t), h=1/5/10/20 actual assessment sessions. The target must be a raw price series; factors may be signals. Percent mode uses the corresponding positive-base return. Complete native target paths in one fixed contract are required before seasonal mapping; missing quotes never shorten h. Common mature samples are used across horizons when sufficient. Otherwise valid shorter horizons remain visible with their own N, and unequal samples are not ranked. Overlapping labels, shared price inputs, multiple comparisons and unknown publication vintages preclude treating the displayed r as proven predictive ability.

Calendar Overlay preserves relative delivery years and matching fixed delivery seasons. Historical-only selections are also cut at the current seasonal position. Spot/Rolling year entries align with a selected fixed contract's reference year offset. Raw continuous Spot/Rolling entries use their latest observed quote year in correlation overlay; explicit year entries select older years. Weekly/monthly overlay bins are formed AFTER mapping daily observations to common seasonal positions; original source dates remain metadata. Periods spanning a contract roll are excluded. Lead/Lag always uses daily native assessment calendars, so Frequency is hidden there.

## Streamlined controls and scientific boundaries

Forecast core controls are Target, BFA Contract, Model (Difference / Ratio / Exploratory), Reference Years, Reference Window and Prediction Horizon. Forecast Through, duplicate Evaluation Horizon, weighting/year-policy/partition controls, score-details popovers and a separate settlement-month selector were removed from the current UI. BFA paths use the remaining contract window; Spot defaults to three months. The selected target determines the integrated Spot settlement month. Exploratory uses the last selected basic model and remembers that choice; subjective strength changes its prospective scenario, never retrospectively inventing factor history. Genuine issue records remain immutable.

The summary compares the target prediction against the latest real quote, never the slope of the prediction curve. It reports current price, forecast, absolute/percentage change and Bullish/Bearish/Flat. Historical direction accuracy is not a probability of this forecast rising. The only default score cards are Direction Accuracy, Forecast Skill Score, MAE and Samples. Majority-direction baselines use the same horizon and past matured observations. Missing outcomes are excluded before counting samples, and roundoff near zero is consistently treated as flat.

Spot and BFA publication calendars are separate. In particular, BFA publishes on some Christmas weekdays when dry Spot indices are closed. Known calendars cover 2026–2027; other future dates remain indicative. Predictions keep real model discontinuities instead of drawing a false link to the current price. Nearest historical matching is limited to three calendar days within the same month and within observed source coverage; datetime precision is normalized before applying that tolerance. February month-end contract matching preserves leap-year month ends.

Expired fixed contracts can be reviewed even when no future path remains. Historical Replay uses current parameters; Saved Forecasts reads issued values, including original engine/calendar assumptions. Evolution uses a fixed target independently of the selected scoring horizon and rejects future non-publication dates. Historical-year options are based on the full available benchmark history rather than an eight-year UI loading cap. Benchmarks such as S10TC/S11TC and C5TC/C5TC (182) are never silently spliced.

## Current unified Forecast interface

Forecast is now split by target: **Spot Forecast** and **BFA Forecast**. Both use the same seasonal Difference/Ratio framework, reference-year/window controls (including Custom), automatic forecast range, chart components, replay and versioned journal. Configurations remain target-specific. Each point pairs available historical future prices and reference means from the same years. Partial year coverage is allowed without filling data; hover reports actual contributors, and membership changes break the drawn line. Spot Forecast integrates monthly settlement estimates from known Spot plus the remaining forecast, only when the publication calendar and full monthly coverage exist; no quarter/year aggregation is inferred. Contract Settlement is no longer a primary UI entry. Legacy settlement/regression implementations remain in source for research compatibility.

Four default score metrics are Direction Accuracy, Forecast Skill Score, MAE and Samples. Skill = 100 × (1 − model MAE / unchanged-price MAE); zero baseline error or inadequate cases suppresses the score. Prediction Horizon is 1D/5D/10D/20D/Custom, default 5 sessions; it controls the target conclusion and same-horizon validation. Fewer than ten finite, non-overlapping outcomes suppresses historical scores. Replay is chronological, with non-overlapping targets, train-time historical-year availability and a separate development/holdout partition. BFA can reuse matched expired peers; timestamp-verified mode rejects unknown vintages. These exploratory scores are not proof of trading profitability.

One review chart switches between Forecast vs Actual, Forecast Error and Forecast Evolution. Historical Replay uses current parameters. Saved Forecasts reads original issued values, never recalculates or overwrites them; it scores only available, non-overlapping outcomes from selected saved versions. Evolution keeps the target date fixed and varies the prediction-origin date, including future targets. It is independent of the fixed Evaluation Horizon and does not run unrelated score calculations. The UI automatically preserves selected historical-year lags at each origin and uses only information then available. Fixed-year and timestamp-gated calculation APIs remain in source for research; their technical switches are not exposed in the streamlined Forecast controls. Subjective scenarios affect future paths and prospective records but are excluded from retrospective skill. Multi-factor linear/Ridge fitting has not yet been integrated into this new shared UI; the prior research implementations are retained. Daily storage reuses the existing private forecast journal and worker for both targets.

The sections below document retained implementations, including the legacy settlement research; they do not imply all legacy controls remain primary UI entries.

Deploy main / app.py in Streamlit Community Cloud. The default uses synthetic data. Live mode uses a private GitHub repository. Configure the GitHub read credential in Streamlit Secrets.

All four modules appear on one page, with global vessel and benchmark selection:

1. **Forecast:** Spot Forecast and BFA Forecast use seasonal Difference/Ratio paths; monthly settlement estimates are integrated into Spot Forecast.
2. **Spot Market:** one Spot / MA5 / MA20 / Bollinger trend chart, plus seasonal comparison. Legend clicks replace redundant line-display buttons.
3. **Forward Market:** one Price Explorer for prices, normalization, differences, ratios and calendar overlays, plus Forward Curve and Repricing views.
4. **Correlation:** a shared Matrix / Rolling / Lead-Lag tool, with the existing Spot/BFA OOS study available on demand.

Charts use the full content width without data tables. Selected views load on demand. Date charts include a plain bottom double-ended draggable range slider with the miniature traces hidden, pan/zoom/reset and stable per-chart UI state. Legends and range buttons occupy separate bands. Every module has a local Refresh that rereads saved quotes and invalidates that module's computations without starting data collection or resetting other modules. Low-frequency date/history settings are in popovers.

Settlement models show both equations and their intermediate values. Published settlement sessions are locked when the verified calendar is available. Quarterly and annual strips are shown by monthly settlement leg. Current dry-index calendars cover 2026 and 2027. Earlier reconstructed actuals are not reconciled to clearinghouse prints. Reference-window comparisons use the early development interval; the recent 30% is reserved for holdout scoring. Fitted exploratory corrections use earlier matured targets and reject highly collinear inputs. Manual factors are subjective scenarios.

Daily curves keep one target month fixed and use only reference data eligible on each forecast date. Compare against same-day BFA, or reconstructed final settlement once the month has matured and sufficient actuals are available. Before settlement, the difference from BFA is a pricing gap, not realized forecast error. Enable Include settled contracts in Advanced Settings to revisit matured targets. Reconstructed curves are labelled separately from saved forecasts; historical fitted or subjective exploratory predictions are shown only where actual saved records exist. Forecast defaults to Latest quote, so new assessments and newly ingested contracts become available without changing a pinned forecast date. Existing valid contract selections are preserved.

Settlement configurations and generated settlement forecasts are saved in the current browser and can be exported. Records include their actual creation time. Replay dates do not generate historical forecast records. The new BFA price-path journal has a separate private daily worker; settlement forecasts are not generated by that worker.

## Future BFA quotation paths

Forecast target → BFA price path projects every indicative future assessment date through the selected contract's expiry. Forecast(t) = historical matching-contract price(t) + A − B, where A is the current fixed contract's latest 20 valid quotes' mean and B is the corresponding historical reference mean. The historical future curve keeps its shape; the mean difference shifts its level. Windows of 5, 10, 20, 30 and 60 quotes and optional recent weighting remain available. Select one or more historical reference years; the default is the latest five eligible years. Historical contracts match the vessel, benchmark, unit, tenor, delivery season and year-shifted delivery interval and must have expired before the forecast origin. Historical reference dates shift by the same whole-year offset. Seasonal point alignment reads the nearest real historical quote within three calendar days in the same month. Per-point historical price and reference means use the same available years. Missing matches remain gaps, with sample counts on hover; no flat-mean fallback is used. Actual BFA is solid, future forecasts are dashed and shaded, and the price/difference views and saved-version reviews use the same chart.

Exploratory price paths add an explicit bullish/bearish judgement and strength. The maximum percentage and effect deadline are configurable; the default fades over the next 20 indicative sessions. Expiry-wide and custom-date effects are also available. A saved judgement's deadline does not restart when the next daily worker runs. Future dates use the dry-index calendar where available, then weekdays; the forward-assessment schedule remains indicative. Missing actual quotes are never filled, and no calibrated probability or unsupported error band is invented.

New seasonal-difference records use engine version bfa-seasonal-difference-v2. Earlier v1 flat-mean records remain immutable and are labelled by their original engine in review. Every issued path records its genuine generation time, data cutoff, fixed contract, input-price revision, complete configuration and future target-date values. Newly issued records exclude dates on or before the issue date. Subsequent actual quotes are joined separately in Saved forecast review; changing a factor never rewrites an issued path. Reference window comparison uses common, non-overlapping development cases at a chosen horizon, the same tenor and exact months to delivery. The recent 30% is excluded from window ranking; historical vintage timestamps remain unverified. A window ranking is withheld if too few common cases exist.

The private data repository runs `forecast_daily.py` after its existing collector. It saves the default seasonal-difference forecasts with a 20-quote reference and up to five eligible historical years for active contracts, plus explicitly activated configurations, into separate `forecasts/` files on the private `ffa-data` branch. It never modifies `market.json.gz`. Append-only record identities and compare-and-retry writes preserve previous issues and concurrent updates. No records are backfilled before the worker's real first run. These code changes must be uploaded to the private repository before daily automation is active.

The dashboard reads the private journal using the existing read credential. Interactive browser records are exportable. An optional Streamlit secret `github_forecast_writer_token` (private data repository Contents read/write) enables the explicit **Save and enable daily updates** action; this saves the current path and registers the configuration for subsequent daily runs. Keep this secret out of source files. Without it, the scheduled default seasonal-difference forecasts still work after the private worker is installed, while interactive scenarios remain browser-only until exported.

Forecast input switches independently between Spot and BFA. In BFA mode, A is the selected monthly contract's BFA reference mean, B is the matching historical monthly contracts' reference mean at the same seasonal position and delivery distance, and C is their final Spot settlement mean. Difference and Ratio formulas remain unchanged. BFA inputs estimate the entire month's settlement; observed Spot is not blended into that estimate a second time. BFA history, formulas and chronological validation are kept separate from Spot mode; insufficient counterpart history is reported instead of substituting another tenor.

Exploratory mode defaults to a simple prospective judgement: Neutral, Bearish or Bullish plus strength from 0 to 1 and an optional reason. The maximum remaining-price adjustment defaults to a subjective 10% and can be changed in Advanced model factors. Bearish strength 0.3 therefore reduces the unobserved portion's base price estimate by 3%; published settlement observations stay fixed. A live Exploratory scenario curve applies the current assumptions across the base snapshots, and both the price and difference views update immediately. This hypothetical sensitivity curve is not a historical forecast record or backtest; saved diamonds retain actual generated forecasts. Judgements are saved as versioned scenarios, excluded from reconstructed historical performance, and are not probabilities or fitted coefficients. Optional quantitative factors and regression controls remain available under Advanced model factors. The result describes settlement above or below current BFA, rather than claiming a next-day BFA direction.

## Spot → BFA validation

Legacy research implementations are retained in source; their separate Daily Changes, Lead/Lag and Relationships navigation is no longer shown. The current fourth module is Correlation. The research views keep each fixed contract separate; only one benchmark/quote unit is allowed. All charts remain full-width, with no added data tables. Long research training history loads only when required and is cached by exact source revisions and scope.

Research defaults to clearly labelled **Date replay**, **Historical peers**, **M+1** and a **3Y** window, so the initial view has useful validation coverage. Select **Selected contract** to inspect one fixed delivery separately. **Verified timestamps** and contract type are available in **Research settings**; methodology is under **Method notes**. Price-history defaults are unchanged. Small-sample thresholds and leakage controls are unchanged.

The research window loads two years of initial training history before the selected validation start, plus the existing calendar warmup, and then expands training forward. It does not load the entire old archive for the default view. Data remain continuous through the latest saved assessment so future-session labels never skip an unloaded calendar interval. Select All history to include the entire available archive.

The lead/lag matrix compares Spot changes at t, t−1, …, t−5 with cumulative BFA changes BFA(t+h)−BFA(t), h=1/3/5 assessment sessions. The calendar is the benchmark's union of observed assessment dates; missing quotes do not compress the horizon. Labels and features stay in one fixed contract. Filters separate months to the delivery-start month. Correlation needs at least 30 pairs; each conditional up probability needs at least 20 observations of that Spot direction, with sample counts and an unconditional up-rate comparison on hover. The 18 exploratory relationships, overlapping future labels and serial dependence preclude treating this matrix as significance or predictive proof.

**Strict PIT and exploratory date replay are separate.** Strict uses the stored vintage only after its received/available timestamp, with a prediction cutoff at 23:59:59.999999999 UTC on the assessment date. Existing historical CSVs lack publication/receipt times and vintage history: those observations are ineligible for strict validation. Exploratory replay assumes date-end availability, is prominently marked as unverified, and never confirms a usable predictive edge. No publication times are invented from quote dates.

Backtests use expanding training windows, with every training label's end date strictly before the prediction date; strict mode additionally checks label availability. The pipeline trains both feature standardization and the fixed Ridge model (alpha=10) on the training set only. Spot features are six absolute daily changes t…t−5; BFA-only has the matching six own changes. Peer training uses only contracts expired at that prediction time, with the same benchmark, unit, tenor and **exact integer months to delivery**. At least 60 distinct training dates and three peers are required. Same-day peer rows share total weight one. The selected contract's OOS score remains separate from historical-cohort validation. Historical cohort validation deterministically selects the earliest-delivery eligible contract per date, never the contract with the best outcome.

Baselines are unchanged price, h times the current BFA change, and a BFA-only Ridge model; the richer Ridge adds Spot features. Scores use identical OOS cases, greedily select nonoverlapping target intervals and require 30 such cases. Direction has three classes (up/flat/down); unchanged price is only an error baseline. Error is mean absolute error in USD/day of the cumulative change. Incremental Spot MAE and direction gains are compared to BFA-only using paired moving-block bootstrap intervals (1,000 draws, fixed seed, block length min(20, floor(n/4)), at least two). These intervals describe uncertainty, not future guarantees. Publication uncertainty or inadequate samples blocks predictive conclusions. No hyperparameter is selected using held-out outcomes; the evaluated date window is not an untouched final holdout for future user experimentation.


## Price Explorer alignment and selection

The inventory loads available historical contracts independently of chart display bounds. Search labels include DEC26, JAN27, Q127 and CAL27, category, delivery year and tenor; active nearby contracts are prioritized. Explicit Spot/Rolling year entries allow quick seasonal comparisons. The same labels are reused in Forecast and research selectors.

Actual Date preserves actual quote dates. Calendar Overlay shifts an entire fixed contract history by its delivery year, so DEC26/DEC25 and JAN27/JAN26 preserve cross-year lead-in periods and overlay at the same seasonal position. Spot/Rolling year entries align by quote year. Original dates remain on hover; differences/ratios compare exact aligned dates with compatible units, then aggregate. Continuous multi-year Spot/Rolling entries require choosing a year for overlay arithmetic. No missing quotes are filled; rolling changes retain breaks and dated markers.
